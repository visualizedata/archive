Little Stones At My Window Sofia Molina

Remix is an experimental web project that explores remix as a creative method. The piece reinterprets a poem by Mario Benedetti, transforming the literary text into a visual and interactive digital experience. Through the combination of typography and motion, the project translates the emotional tone of the poem into a dynamic visual language.
