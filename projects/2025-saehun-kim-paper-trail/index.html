Paper Trail Saehun Kim

This project visualizes how language evolved across more than 400 text-based documents from 1770 to 1810. By classifying each document into topics such as Military, Politics, Society, Business, and Religion, the visualization reveals how different themes coexisted within the same period and how their vocabularies shifted over time. Users can explore patterns across decades through an interactive tiles of archival materials.
