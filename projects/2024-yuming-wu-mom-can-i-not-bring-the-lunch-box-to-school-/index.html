MOM! Can I Not Bring the Lunch Box to School?'

by YuMing Wu

This research focuses on the narratives of second-generation Asian immigrants in the U.S., a rapidly growing demographic. It investigates their cultural struggles and identity formation, straddling Eastern and Western cultures. The project will create a database using qualitative data from various sources, and machine learning techniques will categorize this data to identify key themes. The ultimate aim is to increase understanding of their experiences among the wider population.
